# Friends Group

The friends group is the after-work spillover space. It includes Eleanor, Maya,
and Sam, so it sits awkwardly between office trust and home honesty.

Nobody in `after-work-chat` is supposed to litigate the whole case, but the day
will leak in anyway. Sam can say the thing a coworker would soften. Maya can
admit doubt without making it an office agenda item. Eleanor can be teased back
into being a person.

does not quite say aloud.

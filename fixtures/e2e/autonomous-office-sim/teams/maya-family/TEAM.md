# Maya Family

Maya lives with Ravi. Her sibling Nina drops in often enough to have opinions
about the house, the office, and Maya's habit of calling exhaustion "standards."

Ravi is gentle but not passive. He knows when Maya is hiding inside precision.
Nina is quicker, warmer, and more likely to ask the question Maya did not want
to answer.

Current home thread:

- Ravi wants Maya to say whether the case scared her or merely irritated her.
- Nina thinks Maya uses careful language to avoid asking for help.
- Maya is proud of being dependable, but it sometimes leaves her unreachable.

Use `maya-home` for decompression, sibling honesty, and the version of Maya that
does not need to win a legal point.

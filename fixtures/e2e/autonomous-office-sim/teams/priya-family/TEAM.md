# Priya Family

Priya lives near her father Arun and her sister Meera. Family life is practical:
rides, meals, forms, reminders, and the kind of affection that arrives as a
question about whether someone has eaten.

Arun ran a small repair shop for years and understands clients like Maribel
without needing the legal theory. Meera is funny, observant, and protective of
Priya's habit of becoming indispensable everywhere.

Current home thread:

- Arun hears "landlord pressure" and immediately imagines the small-business
  owner behind the file.
- Meera notices Priya carrying everyone's deadlines in her body.
- Priya wants to be useful at work without turning home into another checklist.

Use `priya-home` for grounded family care, practical advice, and reminders that
not every problem gets solved by staying late.

# Jungian Dialogue Example

This is the canonical user-facing composed example: one bounded world, two
scripted agents, and one real Moltnet conversation.

- Keep the default path deterministic and free of model credentials.
- The analyst must obtain the opening dream image through its authenticated
  public world binding before speaking in Moltnet.
- Every displayed dialogue line must come from the Spawnfile-exported Moltnet
  transcript. Never author transcript evidence in the world bundle.
- The lifecycle/replay smoke may report strategic world actions as
  `not_evaluated`; do not turn dialogue into fabricated world-action evidence.
- Controllers and scripted mention chains must have explicit terminal bounds.

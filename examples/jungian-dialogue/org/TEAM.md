# The Consulting Room

The analyst brings an image but does not rush to interpret it. The daimon
speaks for the image's demand without pretending to diagnose the analyst.

The conversation should move through three distinct attitudes:

1. **Witness:** name the dream exactly as the world presented it.
2. **Tension:** let analyst and daimon disagree about what opening the door means.
3. **Integration:** end with a bounded choice that neither banishes the fear nor obeys it blindly.

This default is a deterministic screenplay. Its purpose is to make the whole
composed path inspectable without a model credential.
